\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{ch:Conclusion} 

\section{Conclusion on the Case Study}

This case study allowed us to apply strategies studied in class to a concrete and self-defined problem. By designing a small-scale drone light show, we connected theoretical concepts such as consensus, formation control, graph-based interactions, and reference tracking with an actual multi-agent application. Even if the real test did not match the simulations perfectly, the show was successfully presented and the final result was satisfactory. This difference between simulation and reality also highlighted an important engineering point: a model can guide the design, but real experiments remain necessary to validate and adjust the system.

\section{Ongoing Work and Perspectives on the Case Study}

From the problem we defined, many different solutions are possible. In practice, the result is mainly limited by the creativity of the show designer, the number of drones, the available space, and the safety constraints. The concepts studied in the course were essential for structuring the work, especially consensus for collective behavior and formation control for geometric patterns. Future improvements could include tuning the controller gains after real flight tests, increasing the available flight area, and using more drones to create richer animations and more complex three-dimensional figures.

\section{Conclusion on the Contribution of the Course}

This project showed that practical work is an effective way to understand more deeply the concepts introduced in class. It required us to build rigorous modeling criteria, translate theoretical ideas into algorithms, and think creatively in order to solve our own problem. The project also improved our understanding of collective behavior in multi-agent systems, especially the importance of communication, local interactions, and coordination rules. Overall, the course provided both the theoretical tools and the practical motivation needed to design, analyze, and implement a coordinated drone show.
